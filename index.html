About STS Logistics

A Sabah-based forwarder that moves businesses forward

STS, established in 2007, is a Sabah-based logistics and freight forwarding company committed to delivering reliable, efficient, and cost-effective supply chain solutions across air, sea, and land.

With strong industry expertise and operational excellence, we support businesses of all sizes in managing logistics with confidence and precision.

Our mission

To provide seamless logistics solutions that empower businesses to grow through efficiency, reliability, and innovation.

Our vision

To be a leading logistics partner in the region, recognized for excellence, trust, and customer satisfaction.

Our commitment

We are dedicated to building long-term partnerships with our clients by consistently delivering high-quality logistics solutions. Our team combines experience, innovation, and a customer-centric approach to ensure your business operations run smoothly and efficiently.
